Rock-Paper-Scissors Robotic Hands

Two 3D-printed robotic hands, each actuated by two motors and controlled with an MSEduino, with logic that chooses which hand to keep to win.

Photo coming soon

What I did

  • 3D printed and assembled two robotic hands, each actuated by two motors and controlled with an MSEduino microcontroller.
  • Programmed the motor control that moves each hand into rock, paper, or scissors gestures.
  • Programmed a start-up routine that selects the strongest pair of gestures for the two hands, avoiding weak pairs such as paper and paper.
  • Developed decision logic that takes the opposing team’s two hands as input and chooses which hand to keep and which to withdraw to win.
  • Integrated motor control and game logic into a single working system.
